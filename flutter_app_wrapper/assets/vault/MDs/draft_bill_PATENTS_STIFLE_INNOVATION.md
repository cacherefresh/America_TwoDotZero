# Innovation Royalty Act of 2026

**A Bill to Replace the Patent System with a Royalty-Based System**

**Section 1: Short Title**  
This Act may be cited as the "Innovation Royalty Act of 2026."

**Section 2: Findings**  
Congress finds that the current patent system often stifles innovation by granting long monopolies that prevent useful inventions from reaching the market.

**Section 3: Automatic Royalty Rights**  
Any inventor who publicly discloses a new, useful, and non-obvious invention shall automatically receive royalty rights for ten (10) years from the date of disclosure.

**Section 4: Six-Month Exclusivity Period**  
The inventor shall have an exclusive six (6) month period to seek investors and commercialize the invention without competition.

**Section 5: Penalty for Shelving Inventions**  
Any company that intentionally shelves an invention to protect existing profits shall be liable for damages. These damages shall include:  
(a) Payment of substantial fines to the federal government,  
(b) Direct compensation to the original inventor equal to lost royalty payments, and  
(c) Compensation to individuals who suffered harm as a direct result of the invention being withheld from the market.  

For example, if a company shelves a medical innovation that could have cured or treated a disease in order to protect profits from existing treatments, that company shall be liable for damages suffered by patients who were denied access to the innovation.

**Section 6: Sliding Scale Royalty**  
Royalty rates shall be determined on a sliding scale between 3% and 25% of net profits. Rates shall be set by an independent board based on the technical complexity of the invention and the commercial difficulty of bringing it to market. The royalty rate shall diminish gradually over the ten-year period.

**Section 7: Rights of the Original Inventor**  
The original inventor retains full rights to use, manufacture, sell, or further develop their invention at any time, even while other companies are using it and paying royalties.
