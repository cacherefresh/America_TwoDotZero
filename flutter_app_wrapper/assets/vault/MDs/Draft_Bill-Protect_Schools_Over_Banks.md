**A bill to require priority armed protection for schools over banks.**

**Section 1.** Any municipality that provides an armed guard at a bank shall be required to provide at least one armed guard at each public school within that municipality.

**Section 2.** The armed guard assigned to the school shall take priority over any armed guard assigned to a bank.

**Section 3.** Any municipality found in violation of this act shall require the bank to fully fund the salary, benefits, training, and equipment of the armed school guard for as long as the bank maintains its own armed security.

**Section 4.** Every municipality covered under this act shall establish a shared School Protection Fund financed by all banking institutions operating within the municipality. Contributions to the fund shall be proportionate to the number of physical bank locations each banking institution operates within the municipality.

For purposes of determining contribution amounts, the total annual cost of armed school protection, including salary, benefits, training, and equipment, shall be divided proportionally among all participating banking institutions according to their share of total bank locations.

**Example:** If the annual cost of school protection is $200,000 and there are:
- 2 locations operated by Bank Company A,
- 1 location operated by Bank Company B,
- 1 location operated by Bank Company C,

then:
- Bank Company A shall contribute $100,000,
- Bank Company B shall contribute $50,000,
- Bank Company C shall contribute $50,000.

**Section 5.** The county sheriff, chief law enforcement officer, and the elected municipal or county governing authority shall jointly develop and approve an annual public safety funding plan to ensure full compliance with this act.

No funds appropriated for:
- teacher salaries,
- classroom instruction,
- student programs,
- school transportation,
- special education,
- school maintenance,
- or existing education budgets

may be reduced, redirected, offset, replaced, or otherwise diminished for the purpose of complying with this act.

All funding required under this act shall be supplemental and shall not replace or reduce any existing education appropriations, tax revenues, or legally dedicated school funding streams.

Any municipality found to have reduced education funding in order to offset compliance costs under this act shall be considered in direct violation of the act and shall be subject to financial penalties equal to twice the amount diverted from education funding.

The intent of this section is to ensure that protection of children is treated as a primary public safety obligation without transferring the financial burden onto students, teachers, or educational quality. Preservation of human life and student safety shall take priority over protection of financial assets and commercial property interests.

**Section 6.** In the event of a bank robbery or security incident, local law enforcement shall not respond until all public schools within the municipality are secured.

**Section 7.** This act shall take effect one year after the date of enactment.
