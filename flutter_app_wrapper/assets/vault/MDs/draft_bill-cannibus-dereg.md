# Cannabis as Produce Act of 2026

**A Bill to Classify Cannabis as Ordinary Agricultural Produce**
//This is the super short version, I'll have to dig up my actual one from years back. //TODO R.C.
  
**Section 1: Short Title**  
This Act may be cited as the "Cannabis as Produce Act of 2026."

**Section 2: Classification and Removal from Controlled Substances**  
Cannabis, in all its forms, is hereby classified as an ordinary agricultural crop equivalent to strawberries or tomatoes. Cannabis is completely removed from the Controlled Substances Act and all federal drug schedules. There shall be no federal restrictions on growing, possessing, or using the plant.

**Section 3: Interstate Commerce**  
Cannabis and cannabis products shall be treated as standard agricultural produce. Interstate transportation and commerce shall be fully permitted under existing USDA regulations for agricultural goods.

**Section 4: State Authority**  
States retain the right to regulate sales, (WE ARE A REPUBLIC AFTER ALL, but give the states TIME to think about it, unlike the undoing of ROE v WADE),particularly to minors. No federal age restrictions or licensing requirements shall apply.

**Section 5: Expungement**  
All federal non-violent cannabis convictions are automatically expunged.
